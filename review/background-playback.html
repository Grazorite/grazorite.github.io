BeatNote: Android background playback

Google Play review demonstration for version 2026.2.0 (4), recorded on an Android emulator using synthetic rehearsal audio.

The user starts playback in BeatNote, returns to the Home screen and opens Android’s media notification. Playback continues outside the app, with visible pause and seek controls. This is the user-initiated audio playback task served by FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK.
